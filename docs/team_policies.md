# Team Policies

## Incidents
Production incidents (severity 1 or 2) must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
Post updates in the incident channel every 30 minutes until resolved.

## Meetings
Tuesday and Thursday mornings are focus blocks. Do not schedule meetings before noon on those days.
Default meeting length is 30 minutes unless the topic needs a design review (60 minutes).

## Code review
Pull requests need one approval from a team member and passing CI before merging.
Reviews should be completed within one business day.
